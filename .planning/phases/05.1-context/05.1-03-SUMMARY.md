# Phase 5.1 Plan 03: Integrated Contextual Lookup

The application and CLI expose occurrence-specific Russian meaning independently from sentence translation, while retaining early offline articles.

## Delivered Behavior

- `lookup/translate.py` carries independent meaning value, provider, pending/error/cache flags and persistence warning. Bounded workers never share SQLite connections. First-useful and meaning-ready times remain separate and numeric.
- `lookup/app.py` propagates the exact OCR or chosen phrase span. One accepted UUID event owns one history increment. Cached or fast meaning persists before the early ready callback; delayed meaning updates only the matching live generation. Dismissed/superseded requests cannot repaint or enrich history.
- `lookup/gui.py` displays separate plain-text meaning and sentence sections, explicit unavailable states and bounded-fragment copy. Late updates retain dictionary widgets, count and inactivity deadline. A separate warning reports meaning persistence errors without losing the article.
- `lookup/__main__.py` passes exact image metadata and maps manual context only when its whole-token occurrence is unique. Repeated ambiguous manual text does not guess a selected occurrence.
- The Phase 6 snapshot remains word, dictionary translation, exact source/span, optional contextual meaning and provider; the contextual update never increments counts or changes lookup timestamps.

## Limits and Deviations

Host production live success and native controlled-response success are separate evidence; no live VM success is claimed.
