## Deviations from Plan

1. **[Rule 1 — Actual OCR paragraph grouping]** Initial VM matrix passed 94/104. Eight failures showed PSM 6 splitting a four-line sentence into two paragraph IDs inside one block. Recovery now permits a tightly aligned lowercase continuation after an unfinished line across that paragraph split. A portable regression rejects an uppercase unrelated paragraph; all column/caption/dialogue checks remain strict. PSM remains 6.
2. **[Fixture calibration; unresolved OCR limit retained]** Two initial failures read line-start `is` as `Is` at 2×, with an unreliable context flag. The controlled repeated-word fixture now uses `stands` to test occurrence mapping independently of this Tesseract casing error. Production does not guess or repair case. The initial evidence is preserved; success of the final matrix does not claim all fonts/text are recognized correctly.
3. **[Rule 2 — Ambiguous punctuation]** Common abbreviations and initials are not treated as reliable sentence delimiters; `Dr. Smith sat on the bank.` remains the full available fragment with `context_complete=False`.
